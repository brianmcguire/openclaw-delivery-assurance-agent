# Record the completed real two-person pilot

The September 27 pilot was performed by a real founder and a different real owner. The project and delivery statements are fictional. A recording does not exist yet. This retrospective recording option shows actual saved interactions; narrate it as a walkthrough of a completed pilot, not newly typed live activity. Do not resubmit the commands merely to generate usage.

## Capture

Get both participants agreement before publishing their contributions. On macOS, press Shift-Command-5, select Record Selected Portion, and frame the chat content. Keep private account emails, invitation links, unrelated windows, notifications, and network administration out of the capture. Record actual scrolling through the completed conversation and the final report. Save the original recording. Target 80-90 seconds, never below 60 seconds.

## Narration and shots

- 0-10 seconds: Show the fictional project title and explain: "Delivery Lens is the first delivery lead for a startup managing customer pilots. This is a walkthrough of a real two-person test using fictional project data."
- 10-22 seconds: Show the founder project setup record, then the two distinct verified identities and owner enrollment. "The founder defines outcomes, milestone owners, dates, and escalation rules. A separate person joins as pilot lead."
- 22-38 seconds: Show the owner update and actual agent recovery question. "The owner reports missing sandbox access and a threatened date. The agent saves the original statement, attributes its source, and asks for a recovery action."
- 38-52 seconds: Show the owners actual recovery reply and agent receipt. "The owner commits to a synthetic dataset by September 29. The agent records the plan and stops repeating the answered recovery question."
- 52-67 seconds: Show the founder brief. "The founder gets amber health, evidence, and an approval decision. A recovery plan is not treated as a resolved blocker or an approved commitment."
- 67-82 seconds: Show the actual standalone CORRECT submission, applied receipt at revision 4, and updated brief. "The owner corrects the target date. The schedule concern resolves while the original update remains in the audit history. Sandbox access remains blocked."
- 82-90 seconds: Show the public MIT repository. "One shared agent keeps delivery evidence current and puts the next decision in front of the founder."
