# Delivery Lens Agent Index screenshots

These three JPEGs are unaltered captures from the completed September 27 OpenClaw pilot. Brian and Jonathan used distinct verified accounts in a shared conversation. The project, milestone, blocker, and recovery plan shown here are explicitly fictional. These images were previously used in the narrated demo; no chat text or agent response was recreated for this package.

1. [Owner update and recovery question](01-owner-update-and-recovery-question.jpg) — Jonathan reports missing sandbox access. Delivery Lens records the source, flags the target date, and asks him for a specific recovery action and date. Brian's founder-brief request is visible below.
2. [Owner recovery and founder follow-up](02-owner-recovery-and-founder-followup.jpg) — Jonathan commits to synthetic test data and asks for approval. Delivery Lens records the plan, keeps the blocker open, and notes the question is answered.
3. [Corrected founder brief](03-corrected-founder-brief.jpg) — The later brief shows amber health, the corrected date, source references, the unresolved blocker, and the founder's pending decision.

The captures retain the OpenClaw interface and participant aliases. They contain no credentials, customer names, real customer records, or private network addresses.
